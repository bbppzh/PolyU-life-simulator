# PolyU Life Simulator V3

An independent text-driven game about four fictional years at PolyU. Choose one of six major directions and two starting strengths, then make 32 decisions across eight semesters. Four dynamic scores—GPA, Sanity, Social Value and Resume Strength—stay hidden during play. Earlier choices influence later scenes. Eight personalised checkpoint endings reveal your results, major-specific next chapter and meaningful choices.

Play: https://bbppzh.github.io/PolyU-life-simulator-V3/

V1 and V2 are preserved as separate websites. This homepage opens directly into V3.

Download `PolyU-Life-Simulator.html` for offline desktop play. All code and styles are embedded; no ChatGPT, AI API, account, external fonts or asset requests are needed. On iPhone, use Safari to open the website, rather than the Files preview. Refreshing or closing resets the story. Character names stay in the page and are not sent to a server.

This is an unofficial fictional simulation. GPA is a simplified game score, not a credit-weighted university transcript; endings do not predict honours, admissions, employment or real graduation eligibility. Major directions are simplified story paths rather than exact programme curricula. Sanity is a fictional game resource.

Scenario context: [add/drop](https://support.ar.polyu.edu.hk/portal/en/kb/subregistration/add-drop-period), [residential education](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/nso/holistic-development/residential-education/), [WIE](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/nso/plan-your-study-2/work-integrated-education/). Specific programme rules differ; the story invents its people, choices and consequences.
