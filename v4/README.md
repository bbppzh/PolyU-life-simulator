[Play V4](https://bbppzh.github.io/PolyU-life-simulator/v4/)

A separate game based on V3.2. V1, V2, V3, V3.1 and V3.2 retain their own pages. V4 keeps six majors, eight talents, 16 attribute points, 32 main moments, three branching follow-ups, eight optional seeded campus surprises, live status, eight semester GPA/CGPA records, eight broad endings, the journal, ending timeline, original pixel soundtrack, interaction sounds, separate volumes, live spectrum and optional feedback link.

## Campus notebook interface

V4 now uses a responsive campus dashboard. On desktop, a student passport, live status, wallet and routine blocks sit beside the central pixel campus scene and story; Jade, Ken, Dr Leung and a 32-moment life map appear in the other column. Phones put the current story first, with the supporting panels below. The original red-brick SVG illustration changes its sky and campus caption with the current story moment; it is an illustration, not a real-time campus feed.

The notebook toolbar opens saved grades, people, budgets and the complete journal without leaving the current decision. Click a lived map square to revisit its actual recorded consequence, or a semester label to review that term's routine, results and decisions. The GPA/CGPA chart shows completed semesters only and keeps current estimates separate. Star a journal memory to filter it into Saved; these bookmarks belong to the current run and clear when a new run begins. Future map cells cannot advance the game. Keyboard shortcuts pause while a notebook or sound dialog is open. Closing the notebook returns focus to its opener when it is still present.

Every panel reads the existing game state; opening records, viewing charts and saving memories do not consume time, spend money, change scores or reroll outcomes. Existing background choices, routines, narrative branches, characters, grades, seeds, music/effects controls, live spectrum, graduation comparison and optional feedback remain. Gameplay and all illustrations remain self-contained in the offline HTML.

## Different starting points

Choose local or international student, hall or commuting, and tight/steady/comfortable monthly living support. Background changes time and money; it does not set academic ability. The four attributes and your decisions still shape that.

All money is fictional HKD after tuition and accommodation. Every term represents four months. Support is $1,800 / $2,800 / $4,200 per month, and savings start at $600. Hall daily living costs $7,200 per term; commuter living and transport costs $5,200. International setup adds $600 in the first term and distant connections cost $400 per term. These are game values, not actual university prices, entitlements or employment rules.

Hall residents have 12 flexible weekly time blocks; commuters reserve two for travel and allocate ten. The commuter route meets Jade as a campus friend, with lunch, departure times and shared-campus-space conflicts instead of giving the commuter a fictional room in hall. Social story choices cost $160 in hall or $220 when commuting; career-tagged choices add $180. Social commitments add one Sanity of journey fatigue for commuters. Costs are visible before choosing and saved in the journal. Campus surprises can be skipped for no added cost or stat effect.

## Eight semester routines

Before each semester, allocate every available block among Study, Social, Part-time work and Rest. The game shows a complete budget forecast and score effects before committing. Balanced, study, work and recovery presets are editable. No story decision is accepted before the routine is committed; each term can be committed only once.

Study changes the starting GPA estimate by 0.10 per block above/below three. Rest changes Sanity by three per block above/below two. Social changes connection points by two per block above/below two. Each work block provides $900 per term and 0.8 Resume. More than seven combined Study/Work blocks costs four Sanity. The term forecast credits support and work, then deducts essentials and background costs together. Story spending changes this balance afterward. Unspent money and shortfalls carry forward.

A projected shortfall costs four Sanity at planning. Further spending while short adds up to four Sanity of financial pressure per choice; the exact result is recorded. A shortfall never locks a player out. The eight-routine history preserves allocations, opening balances, costs and story spending. Equal-weight semester GPA/CGPA rules and the final-exam grade lock remain.

## Same intake, different lives

Jade balances community work and paid shifts. Ken balances practical projects and family responsibilities. Each has eight written semester chapters, independent seeded priorities, simulated grades, wellbeing and practical experience. Their choices advance once per completed semester, including after the last farewell. They use a separate seed stream from player interviews and campus-event selection. Supportive shared conversations can add a small wellbeing and academic benefit, while their own responsibilities continue.

At the next semester's planning screen, their latest chapter is visible. Graduation shows the player, Jade and Ken side by side, with CGPA, Sanity, Resume and individual next routes. Each classmate's full eight-semester history can be expanded. This comparison is a story about different lives, rather than a leaderboard. Dr Leung and all existing relationship memories remain.

## Replay and controls

The same seed, background, major, talents, attributes, routines and story choices reproduce a run. Replay this build & seed preserves the starting setup; repeat your routines and choices to recreate it. There are 35 core story decisions, or 43 with campus surprises, plus eight routine plans. Numeric keys choose story cards, and Enter continues outcomes. Routine controls, background radios, sound settings and timeline controls support keyboard focus and touch. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Download `PolyU-Life-Simulator.html` for a self-contained desktop edition. It includes the game code, styles, font and synthesized audio; gameplay uses no login, API, model tokens or external service. The optional Microsoft Forms feedback link needs internet. A red-brick pixel button in the bottom-right opens [PolyU Campus 3D](https://bbppzh.github.io/polyu-campus-3d/) in a separate tab, preserving the current game page. The campus link also needs internet and is included in the offline edition. On iPhone use the public website in Safari. Refreshing or closing resets this run; sound preferences are stored separately.
