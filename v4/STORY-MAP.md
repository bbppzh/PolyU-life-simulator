- The latest teamwork agreement drives the later team scene. A first-year rescue may coexist with a changed approach, with one coherent callback.
- Every internship-interview route has an uncertain outcome. A rejection changes the placement scene to a revised application, supervised local project or bounded alternative placement. The result explicitly confirms that fictional arrangement.
- Exchange/research/local choices change the third-year major-scene context, mentoring callback, career-fair contact and epilogue.
- The 24 major scenes retain their discipline-specific premises with 72 distinct result paragraphs, varied effects and defensible motives.
- Scope and feedback return at final submission; hall arrangements return at the reunion.
- GPA/CGPA are locked after the final exams, before next-step and farewell choices. Those scenes have no academic effects.

## V4 context and semester layer

Before each term, commit exactly one routine. Background determines 10 commuting or 12 hall blocks and a four-month fictional living budget. The routine changes starting GPA, Sanity, Social and Resume before the main scene. Decisions can spend the projected balance; their exact living costs and journey fatigue are shown before confirmation. Debt carries forward without blocking play.

Commuter hall entry/conflict and attached follow-ups become shared-campus-space conversations. Their state flags continue to feed later callbacks, with room language adapted to a commuter's context. Jade is a hall/campus friend rather than an assumed roommate.

After each complete semester (step 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31), Jade and Ken each receive a new independent seeded chapter. The final academic checkpoint remains step 29; peer term eight closes after farewell, preserving the final relationship choices. Eight routine histories, eight peer chapters per character and the three-person graduation comparison read the saved states without rerolling them.
